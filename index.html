TripIN

Plan your trip across India

Pick any of the 28 states or 8 union territories to see its must-visit places. Each place shows a short description, opening and closing hours, and its best time of day, plus permits and seasonal closures. Tick the ones you want, set how many days you have, and your plan builds itself. You can mix places from several states.

    Your plan

    Places are grouped in the order you see them, not by distance. Check the map and travel time between them before you book.

    Permit rules, festival dates, and opening days change, so confirm with the official tourism site or forest office before you book.
